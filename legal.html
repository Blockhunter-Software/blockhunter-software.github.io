Blockhunter Software respects intellectual property rights. We respond promptly to valid notices of alleged copyright infringement in accordance with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA), 17 U.S.C. § 512, and equivalent provisions under applicable international law.

01 Filing a Takedown Notice

If you believe that content available through our Services infringes your copyright, you may submit a written notification to our designated agent. Your notice must include all of the following elements, as required under 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3):

  1. A physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner, or a person authorized to act on their behalf.
  2. Identification of the copyrighted work or works claimed to have been infringed. If multiple works are covered by a single notification, a representative list is acceptable.
  3. Identification of the material that is claimed to be infringing or to be the subject of infringing activity, and information reasonably sufficient to permit us to locate the material (such as a URL or specific description).
  4. Your contact information, including your full name, mailing address, telephone number, and an email address at which you can be reached.
  5. A statement that you have a good faith belief that use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  6. A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notification is accurate, and that you are the copyright owner or are authorized to act on behalf of the owner.

Warning: under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f), any person who knowingly materially misrepresents that material is infringing may be subject to liability for damages. Ensure your claim is accurate before submitting.

Submit all DMCA notices to our designated agent via email. We do not accept takedown notices by post, phone, or social media. Email is the sole valid submission channel.

03 Our Response Process

Upon receipt of a complete and valid takedown notice, we will:

  • Notify the user who submitted the content of the takedown, providing them a copy of the notice (with your personal contact information redacted as permitted by law).
  • Inform the affected user of their right to submit a counter-notification.

Incomplete notices — those missing any required element — will not be processed. We will notify the submitter of any deficiency where reasonably practical.

04 Counter-Notification

If you believe your content was removed or disabled as a result of a mistake or misidentification, you may submit a counter-notification. A valid counter-notification must contain, pursuant to 17 U.S.C. § 512(g)(3):

  • Your physical or electronic signature.
  • Identification of the material that was removed and the location at which it appeared before removal.
  • A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good faith belief the material was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification.
  • Your full name, address, and telephone number, and a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the federal district court for the judicial district where your address is located (or, if outside the United States, to any judicial district in which Blockhunter Software may be found), and that you will accept service of process from the party who submitted the original notice.

Upon receipt of a valid counter-notification, we will forward it to the original complainant and may restore the removed content within 10 to 14 business days, unless the complainant files an action seeking a court order during that period.

In appropriate circumstances and at our sole discretion, Blockhunter Software will disable or terminate the accounts of users who are found to be repeat infringers of intellectual property rights.

We define a repeat infringer as a user against whom we have received multiple valid, confirmed DMCA takedown notices within any rolling 12-month period, or a user who demonstrates a pattern of infringing behaviour.
